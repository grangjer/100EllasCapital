Your Impact Investment Roadmap

A 5-step guide to evaluate opportunities and build an impact thesis with clarity and confidence.

Click each step on the roadmap to explore key questions and insights
1
Problem
  • What is the real problem?
  • Who is most affected?
  • Why has it persisted?
Look for evidence, not assumptions. A real problem has data behind it.
2
Solution
  • Does it address the root cause?
  • Who tried before — and why did they fail?
  • Could it cause unintended harm?
Good solutions are relevant, differentiated, and get more accessible as they scale — not less.
3
Strategy
  • Is go-to-market reaching those who need it most?
  • Does the team have domain expertise to execute?
  • How will it sustain itself financially?
  • What are the key execution risks?
A great solution aimed at the wrong market is not investable.
4
Desired Outcome
  • What change will this create?
  • Would this happen without this company?
  • What if the company is acquired?
Define impact before you invest — in the beneficiary's words, not the founder's.
5
Evaluate Results
  • Is impact trajectory moving in the right direction?
  • Has the beneficiary population drifted?
  • What did this teach you for the next deal?
Track what matters. Learn. Adapt. Your portfolio is an evidence base, not just a ledger.
